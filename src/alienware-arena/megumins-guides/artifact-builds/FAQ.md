# 🏺 Artifacts FAQ

Mirrored from Megumin's [【Artifacts】Info](https://www.alienwarearena.com/ucf/show/2167784) Q&A (plus Discord calendar note).

## Time on Site (Ba'li Arches / Gamers Wanted)

> ### I don't get Time on Site Bonus ARP
>
> `🔻`**Use Artifact before maxing 5 ARP** _(Base)_ for Time on Site
> `🔻`Extra ARP after that still works, but **takes extra time**
> `🔻`Check [ARP Log](https://na.alienwarearena.com/account/arp-log) for the real amount
> `🔻`Keep the AWA tab active

## Daily Calendar (Chai Stones)

> ### I don't get Daily Calendar Bonus ARP
>
> `🔻`**Use Artifact before 00:00 UTC** of each day.
>
> ### Will Day 29-30-31 get Artifact Bonus?
>
> `🔻`It'll be **shown on [ARP Log](<https://na.alienwarearena.com/account/arp-log>)** (not always on Control Center)
>
> ### Does it boost anything else?
>
> `🔻`Anything **Calendar related**, like Daily Login Events.

## Steam Quests (Pn 295 Battery / Sylphin) & Community Events

> ### I don't get Steam Quests Bonus ARP
>
> `🔻`**Equip Artifact before** starting any Steam Quests.
> `🔻`Control Center still shows **15 or 25** even with the artifact on
> `🔻`Check [ARP Log](<https://na.alienwarearena.com/account/arp-log>) for the **real ARP**
>
> ### Will Steam artifacts boost Community Event rewards?
>
> `🔻`Nope. **Only H`erkow Plasma Chamber or Zorathian Renaissance Artifact Set**

## Flux (Community Event Playtime)

> ### Will Flux boost Community Event ARP?
>
> `🔻`**Only Playtime affected, nothing else**.

## Market Discount (Light Warping / Mysterious Text / Omniversal Override / Stanley set)

> ### I don't see any discount despite having them equipped
>
> `🔻`The whole ultimate answer for all AWAverse... **LOGOUT-RELOGIN!**
>
> ### Does it work with Game Vault?
>
> `🔻`Yes — Game Vault is part of Marketplace.

## Stuck 24h slot lock

> ### It's been more than 24 hours and I can't switch artifacts
>
> `🔻`**Upgrade any Artifact** (including a maxed one)
> `🔻`**No fragments spent**
> `🔻`H'erkow Warrior Script (starter) or any Interstellar piece works
